## Tagline Options

**Primary tagline:**
> Lost traq? Retraq.

**Concept:** The name "Retraq" plays on "retrace your tracks" — going back over where you've been. The tagline uses "lost track" (a phrase people already say) with the brand spelling, and answers it with the app name. The name explains itself.

**Alternatives:**
- Retrace your days, one traq at a time.
- Traq the moment. Retraq the memory.
- Don't lose traq. Retraq.

## App Store Listing — iOS / Google Play

### App Name
**Retraq™ — Remember Your Week**

### Subtitle (iOS, 30 chars)
`Quick daily jots. Never blank.`

### Short Description (Google Play, 80 chars)
`Jot down your day in seconds. Never draw a blank when someone asks what you've been up to.`

### Full Description

"How've you been? What have you been up to?"

Simple questions. Completely blank mind.

You *did* things this week. You went places, saw people, had moments. But when someone asks, your brain just serves you nothing. It's not that your life is boring — it's that your memory didn't bother saving it.

Retraq fixes that. Open the app, jot down anything — a sentence, a word, a few bullet points — and move on with your day. Takes seconds. When you need to remember, scroll back. That's it.

Anything is a start. "Coffee with Sam." "Dentist." "Good day." That's a traq. Done.

**Simple by design. Not simple by accident.**

Retraq opens clean. No setup wizard. No twenty questions before you can start. Just a space to write. Because if it's not quick, you won't use it. And if you won't use it, what's the point?

Then, over time, if you *want* more — it's all there waiting in settings. Turn on features when they feel right:

- Mood selection — tag how you're feeling
- Entry titles — name your traqs
- Bionic reading — easier scanning, less re-reading
- AM/PM tags — split your day into morning and afternoon
- Yesterday mode — forgot to log? Enter for yesterday instead
- Bullet points — structured entries when you want them
- Text search — find that thing you did three weeks ago
- Weekly roundup — a summary at the end of each week
- Customisable week start — Monday, Sunday, whatever works

Browse by day, week, or month. Search your traqs. Look back and realise you've actually been up to quite a lot.

**Your memory backup. Open in seconds. Done in seconds.**

### Longer social posts

**The problem:**
> Someone asks "what've you been up to?" and your brain gives you absolutely nothing. You know you did stuff. You went places. Things happened. But somehow none of it stuck. Retraq is a dead-simple daily log. Jot down anything — a sentence, a word, even just "good day" — and when you need to remember, scroll back. No templates, no prompts, no pressure. Just a quick note and done.

**The simplicity angle:**
> Most journalling apps dump every feature on you at once and hope you figure it out. Retraq does the opposite. It opens with nothing — just a space to write. Want mood tracking? Turn it on in settings. Want bullet points? Turn them on. Want bionic reading? It's there when you need it. Every feature is off by default because you shouldn't have to fight your own app just to write a sentence. You build it around your brain, not the other way around.

**The speed angle:**
> You know why most people stop journalling? It takes too long. Opening the app feels like a commitment. Retraq takes seconds. Open, jot, done. No decisions to make, no blanks to fill in, no minimum word count. "Had pasta. Watched a film. Forgot to call Mum." That's a traq. That's enough. And next week when someone asks what you've been up to, you'll actually know.

**The accessibility angle:**
> Retraq is built for anyone who needs a simple way to remember their days. Whether you've got a busy brain, a packed schedule, or a memory that just doesn't hold onto the small stuff — it's the same app, the same simplicity. Jot anything. Scroll back when you need to. It's designed to be accessible to everyone, with features like bionic reading and a clean, distraction-free interface that gets out of your way.

**The "anything counts" angle:**
> "Dentist at 2." "Sam's birthday drinks." "Rough day." "Finally fixed the shelf."
>
> That's four traqs. Took about 15 seconds total. And now you'll remember this week actually happened. That's Retraq. Anything is a start.

## Accessibility & Inclusivity Notes

Retraq is designed to be usable by anyone who struggles to remember their day-to-day, for any reason — busy schedules, forgetful brains, cognitive differences, age-related memory changes, or simply not being a "journal person." The copy should never assume *why* someone needs it. Just acknowledge that they do and make it feel easy.

**Copy principles:**
- Never clinical, never medical, never diagnostic
- Speak *with* people, not *at* them
- Humour is fine — self-deprecating warmth, not jokes about conditions
- Assume intelligence, never patronise
- "Simple" means respectful of your time, not dumbed down
- The word "just" is your friend — "just jot something down," "just a sentence"
- Always lower the bar — "even one word counts," "anything is a start"

## Design Notes

### The Q
The letter Q in Retraq can double as a visual motif:
- Looks like a pin/marker on a timeline
- Looks like a speech bubble with a tail
- Looks like a loop looking backward
- Could be the app icon itself — a stylised Q

### Colour & design direction
- Warm, calm tones — not clinical or medical
- Nothing too bright or overstimulating
- Clean, lots of whitespace, zero clutter
- Large tap targets, clear typography
- Consider dark mode as a default option
- Bionic reading as an accessibility feature, not a gimmick

### Palette

Primary identity stays forest-green + mint (see
`lib/theme/app_colors.dart`). v1 + v1.1 introduces a single
distinctive accent hue (Task 13.3):

- **`#33694B` forest green** — primary tokens (CTAs, brand
  surfaces, primary ColorScheme). Communicates calm authority.
- **`#B5F0C9` mint** — primary container / inversePrimary
  (dark mode). Supporting fill for the primary.
- **`#F8FAF8` off-white** — surface (light mode).
- **Retraq mood teal-green scale (Task 13.2)** — custom 5-step
  progression rooted in the brand primary (hue shifted ~18°
  closer to brand green vs the previous unmodified Material
  Teal). Hex values live in
  `lib/theme/app_colors.dart::moodColorsLight` +
  `moodColorsDark`. NEVER reuse for CTAs; each mood colour
  carries specific semantic meaning. Summary:

  | Mood | Light mode | Dark mode | Meaning |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Rough    | `#A4CAB9` | `#B9D6C6` | low energy, muted sage |
  | Low      | `#7FB49F` | `#95BFAA` | subdued teal-green |
  | Neutral  | `#569A80` | `#6FAB92` | centered |
  | Good     | `#347A65` | `#4F9078` | forward, stable |
  | Amazing  | `#1A5D49` | `#34785C` | saturated deep peak |

  **WCAG contrast (verified):** `moodAmazing` with white text
  → ~8.4:1 ✓; `moodRough` with onSurface `#2D3432` → ~6.2:1
  ✓; all intermediate steps pass 4.5:1 against both text
  tokens.
  **Colour-blind fallback:** deutan + protan viewers collapse
  adjacent steps into similar-looking pairs; the UI always
  pairs the blob with a localised label (moodRough /
  moodLow / ...) so the semantic distinction survives via
  text.
- **`#E8A838` warm amber (accent)** — Task 13.3 distinctive
  second colour. Used sparingly (max one accent per screen)
  for:
  - Milestone celebration chips (`_MilestoneChip` in
    `save_success_overlay.dart`)
  - Active-streak flame icon in the Home `ProgressBar`
    (`hasStreak ? accentAmber : cs.primary`)
  - Future: paywall CTA (v1.1 Retraq Pro) and optional
    A/B-tested hero Save button variant
- **NEVER** use amber as a destructive-action color (that's
  `cs.error`) or stack it on `cs.primary` (pick one).
- **Dark-mode variant:** `#EFBF6B` — same hue, lower
  saturation so it sits comfortably on the dark surface
  without over-saturating.
- **`onAccentAmber` = `#2D1F00`** — dark amber text passes
  WCAG 4.5:1 (~9.1:1 measured) when placed on the accent
  amber fill.

If you find yourself using the amber outside the above list,
check Task 13.3 first — the scarcity is what makes the colour
distinctive. A second amber surface dilutes the first.

### Brand voice
- Casual, warm, lightly self-deprecating
- Never preachy about journalling or self-improvement
- Acknowledges the problem without making it heavy
- Short sentences. Low friction. Like the app itself.
- "Quick enough for busy brains. Simple enough for everyone."

## Trademark Usage

**Retraq™** is an unregistered trademark claimed under common law.

### When to use ™
- First prominent mention of **Retraq** on each page, screen, or document (logo/wordmark, header, copyright)
- Copyright lines: "© 2026 Retraq™"
- Legal definitions: "Retraq™ ("the App")"
- App store listing name

### When NOT to use ™
- Subsequent mentions in the same page/document (one ™ per page is enough)
- The verb form: "retraq your week," "your week's been retraqd"
- Technical identifiers: `com.retraq.app`, `package:retraq`, `retraq.sqlite`
- Email addresses: `contact@retraq.app`
- Domain names in URLs: `retraq.app`
- Browser `<title>` tags, `<meta>` descriptions, or `alt` text
- Inside code, filenames, or import paths
