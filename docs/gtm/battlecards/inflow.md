# Inflow battlecard — complement, not replacement

**Status:** Ship-ready · **Task:** 5.6 · **Last updated:** 2026-04-16

> **TL;DR:** Inflow ($22.49–$47.99/mo) does ADHD CBT therapy.
> Retraq ($0 / v1.1 Pro one-time) does ADHD memory capture. They
> are **complements**. Do NOT position Retraq as a replacement for
> CBT — that's a medical-device claim we cannot make.

Pair with the positioning one-pager and the anti-clinical-language
writing rules. This card exists because Inflow is 7–16x Retraq's
price, which is a concrete acquisition story for ADHD users who
have been priced out of structured CBT apps.

## The competitor at a glance

| Field | Inflow |
|---|---|
| Install base | 500K+ (Play) |
| Rating | 4.4★ Play, 4.6★ iOS |
| Price model | Subscription: $22.49–$47.99/mo depending on plan |
| Core pitch | "ADHD CBT therapy app built by clinicians" |
| Platforms | iOS + Android |
| Moat | Clinician-authored content + CBT structure |

## Strengths — do not try to out-compete on these

- **Clinically authored content.** Inflow's modules are written by
  ADHD-specialist clinicians. We cannot replicate this without a
  medical-advisory board (not in v1 scope).
- **CBT structure.** Guided thought-record forms, behavior-chain
  tracking, cognitive-restructuring exercises. Specific, evidence-
  based interventions.
- **Price anchor above us.** Inflow at $22–48/mo is the reference
  point. Users who pay that price expect a clinical product; they
  won't cross-shop Retraq as a replacement, which is fine.

## The wedge — complement, not replacement

Retraq's story vs Inflow is simple: **two different jobs-to-be-done,
two different price points.**

- **Inflow job:** "Teach me CBT techniques to manage ADHD symptoms."
- **Retraq job:** "Help me remember the week I just had because my
  brain won't."

An ADHD adult doing CBT work in Inflow *also* forgets what happened
Tuesday by Thursday. Retraq captures the data an Inflow thought-
record needs as raw material. The pitch writes itself:

> "Capture your day in Retraq for $0 (or a one-time $5.99 Pro).
> Do the CBT work in Inflow for $22.49+/mo. They complement each
> other — the memory backup feeds the therapy, the therapy isn't
> the memory backup."

## When Retraq wins — three acquisition scenarios

### Scenario A — "I can't afford Inflow"

- User sees Inflow's pricing, balks, looks for a free alternative
  to at least capture their day.
- **Creator DM / Reddit comment copy:** "If Inflow's price is
  out of reach right now, Retraq is a free-forever journal built
  around ADHD-shaped memory. It won't replace therapy, but it's
  a solid way to capture the day-to-day when structured CBT
  isn't on the table."

### Scenario B — "I'm in Inflow but I lose track of what to log"

- User is doing CBT work but the thought-record forms require
  context ("What happened? What thought came up? How did you
  feel?") and they can't remember by the time they sit down to
  log.
- **Creator DM copy:** "Retraq is the thing I open 90 seconds
  after something happens to jot the raw moment. Later, when
  I'm doing the Inflow thought-record, I have material to work
  with. They're designed for different time scales."

### Scenario C — "I graduated Inflow and still want to capture
### my week"

- User finished their Inflow program, doesn't need active CBT
  guidance, but wants to keep journaling without the $22+/mo
  commitment.
- **Creator DM copy:** "Once you're past the active CBT phase,
  Retraq keeps the capture habit going at zero ongoing cost.
  It's the journal half of what Inflow was doing, without the
  therapy half you don't need anymore."

## Objection handling

### "Isn't Inflow better because it's clinical?"

> For CBT work, yes. Retraq isn't a therapy app — it's a journal
> that happens to respect ADHD-shaped memory. The two aren't in
> competition; they sit at different price points for different
> jobs.

### "Does Retraq have ADHD-specific exercises?"

> No. We're capture-focused, not intervention-focused. That's
> why we're $0 free (or $5.99 one-time Pro) and Inflow is
> $22.49+/mo — we solve a different, smaller problem.

### "Can I replace Inflow with Retraq?"

> No, and we wouldn't encourage that. If you're getting real
> clinical benefit from Inflow's CBT content, keep it. Retraq
> sits alongside, capturing the week-by-week raw material.
> Replacing therapy with a journal isn't what this is.

## What creators / the team should NOT do

- Do NOT claim Retraq provides "ADHD therapy" or "CBT" or "clinical
  benefit." Those are medical claims Retraq cannot make (see
  Task 9.8 medical-device guardrail).
- Do NOT disparage Inflow's pricing. "7-16x more expensive" is
  factual; "overpriced" is an attack that makes r/ADHD mods
  remove the comment.
- Do NOT file Retraq under the same App Store category as Inflow
  (Health & Fitness / Medical). Retraq is Lifestyle / Productivity
  (see Task 9.4).
