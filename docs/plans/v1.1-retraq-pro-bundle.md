# Retraq Pro — v1.1 one-time IAP bundle spec

**Status:** Draft for founder review · **Task:** 4.9 · **Last updated:** 2026-04-18

v1 ships fully free (no paywall, no gated features) per the April 2026
feature-decisions memo. This doc fills the "nothing to upsell" gap
flagged in the 2026-04-16 audit (Agent 4) by defining the v1.1
Retraq Pro bundle, its dependency chain, and the decision gates
required before it can ship.

## One-sentence positioning

**Retraq Pro** is a one-time $5.99 unlock that layers three
"power-user ergonomics for your Traqs" on top of the free app —
AI recap on device, CSV export, yearly recap card — plus a
warm-palette theme pack as an included sweetener. Lifetime
unlock; no subscription.

Why the framing matters: the ADHD market is subscription-fatigued
(Agent 5 competitive research). A one-time lifetime unlock
signals "we are not a tax" and inverts the typical journaling-app
extraction loop. The cloud AI subscription tier stays SEPARATE
from Pro — see [Part II.1 of the master plan](../../implementation_plan.md#ii1-monetization-model-v11)
for the two-SKU split.

## The 4 candidate features (+1 sweetener), scored

| # | Feature | Dev effort | A5-claimed value | Privacy-story alignment | Activation-gate risk | Include in Pro? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Gemini Nano weekly recap (on-device)** | L | High (top ADHD-fit feature) | High (stays local — privacy pillar intact) | Low (tangible, inspectable) | **YES** — headline |
| 2 | **CSV export** (Plus JSON already free) | S | Medium (power users, therapists) | High | Low (easy to explain) | **YES** — easy win |
| 3 | **"Your Year in Traqs" yearly recap card** | M | High (anniversary moment = word-of-mouth) | High (on-device) | Low | **YES** — retention hook |
| 4 | **Advanced analytics (mood trends × day-of-week, streak heat-map)** | M | Medium (quantitative users only) | High | Medium (may feel too numbers-y for ADHD audience) | **NO** — park for v1.2 |
| 5 | **Theme pack (3 premium palettes, warm accents)** | S | Low alone, Medium as sweetener | High | Low | **Included** as sweetener |

**Selection rationale.** A "value-per-effort" ranking (Nano > CSV > yearly-recap > analytics > themes) plus the A5 warning that the ADHD market rewards *tangible* unlocks over *quantitative* ones pushes advanced analytics out of the v1.1 bundle. Themes alone are too shallow to convert a user, but bundled as an "also included" sweetener they raise perceived value at zero counter-positioning risk.

## Bundle spec — "Retraq Pro v1.1"

### What the user gets

1. **AI recap (weekly), on-device via Gemini Nano**
   - Runs only on Pixel 8+ / supported Tensor devices (hardware fallback policy below).
   - Surface: Recap screen → "Generate AI recap" button (replaces the template-only recap for Pro users).
   - Privacy posture: 0 bytes leave the device. Uses the same Art. 9 explicit-consent row as the cloud tier (Task 12.4), but consent copy is simpler: "Your device generates this — no servers involved."
2. **CSV export**
   - Settings → Data → "Export as CSV" (alongside existing JSON export).
   - Schema: one row per entry, columns = `date`, `label`, `title`, `body`, `mood`, `created_at`, `edited_at`. No FTS internals, no IDs.
   - Opt-in plaintext warning dialog (same pattern as the JSON export from Task 3.9).
3. **Year in Traqs yearly recap card**
   - Triggers on the 12-month install anniversary.
   - Produces a shareable PNG summary (total entries, longest streak, most active weekday, top mood shown as a sparkline). Runs entirely on device.
   - Reuses the existing share-card renderer infrastructure from Task 5.9 / share-card-visual-brief.
4. **Included sweetener: 3 premium theme palettes** (warm-amber, deep-forest, plum-dusk).
   - Per the Task 13.2 custom palette work plus the Task 13.3 warm-accent direction.

### What the user does NOT get

Per the Part II.1 monetization model, these stay on a separate subscription SKU (Retraq Cloud Recap, $2.99/mo or $24.99/yr):

- Cloud-inference AI recap (Anthropic / Gemini / OpenAI model)
- Cloud sync across devices (deferred to v2)
- Family sharing / caregiver view (Part III v2 scope)

This separation is deliberate: Pro is a tangible one-time unlock; Cloud is the opt-in "power users who want cloud polish" tier. Bundling them would muddy the anti-subscription-fatigue pitch.

## Pricing

**$5.99 USD one-time** (region-adjusted via RevenueCat product tiers). See [`gtm/lifetime-sku-decision.md`](../gtm/lifetime-sku-decision.md) for the full rationale — this is the existing Task 5.14 decision; reaffirmed here.

Explicitly LEFT AS TBD until 4 weeks post v1 launch:

- Whether to offer a $3.99 "first week" introductory price
- Whether to expose a family-shareable upgrade path (multiple devices, one purchase) — probably no, since there's no cloud sync, so per-device purchases are already the honest default

## Store-side requirements (v1.1)

All of [`docs/store-listing/submission-checklist.md`](../store-listing/submission-checklist.md) v1.1 "RevenueCat" triggers apply. Additionally:

- Paywall screen copy MUST NOT promise specific memory/health outcomes (forbidden-claims lint, Task 12.5).
- Age rating: 4+ / Everyone unchanged (on-device AI stays within the same category as the free app).
- Privacy nutrition label: v1.1 rewrite per [`privacy-labels-v1.1.md`](../store-listing/privacy-labels-v1.1.md) Financial Info → Purchases row. User Content remains "Data Not Collected" for the Nano path (nothing leaves the device).
- Apple 3.1.2(a) subscription disclosures do NOT apply (one-time IAP) but 3.1.1 "Restore Purchases" DOES — RevenueCat handles that out-of-the-box.

## Analytics hooks (v1.1 only, on-device)

Feeds Epic 11 analytics; all local, no network egress.

- `FeatureCounterKey.proFeatureAttempted` — bumps when a free user taps a Pro-gated surface (paywall impression rate)
- `FeatureCounterKey.nanoRecapGenerated` — per-user count of completed Nano recaps (utilisation rate)
- `proPurchasedAt` (epoch millis, null if free) — delta vs install date drives TTP (time-to-purchase) cohort
- `recap_feedback.thumbs` (Task 11.6) — primary quality signal on Nano output

## Decision gates before v1.1 kickoff

1. **Founder review of this spec.** The bundle composition (Nano + CSV + yearly recap + theme sweetener) is a product decision, not a technical one. Sign off or swap candidates.
2. **B-2 RevenueCat account + products configured.** Products named `retraq_pro_lifetime` (iOS + Android SKUs).
3. **Gemini Nano device-availability matrix.** Which Android devices actually expose the Nano API? Pixel 8/9/10 confirmed; Samsung flagships TBD. The hardware fallback policy depends on the answer.
4. **Art. 9 consent copy review** with privacy counsel (B-9). On-device processing weakens the Art. 9(2)(a) case but the copy still needs to be defensible.
5. **A5 re-check.** If the competitive landscape shifts (e.g. Day One bundles AI into Plus tier cheaply, or Daylio launches a CSV competitor), the Pro value-prop recalibrates.
